## Hi there 👋

Layoff Studio is an independent game development studio founded by industry veterans, focused on creating engaging, accessible, and memorable gaming experiences for a global audience. We specialize in developing casual, party, and multiplayer games using Unreal Engine, combining strong technical expertise with lean and agile production processes.

Our approach is built around efficiency, iteration, and creative freedom, allowing us to transform ambitious ideas into polished experiences while maintaining a focused and sustainable development pipeline. We believe great games come from the balance between solid engineering, compelling design, and a clear understanding of what makes players have fun.
